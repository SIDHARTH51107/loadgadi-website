Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Homelayer Infra Pvt. Ltd., Patna, Bihar, India

Load Gadi is an app and website that connects buyers of balu (sand), gitti (stone chips) and bricks with local suppliers in Bihar, operated by Homelayer Infra Pvt. Ltd.. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Information we collect

  • Name and mobile number of buyers and suppliers, used to log in with a one-time password (OTP) and to contact you about orders.
  • Order details: material, quantity, delivery address, landmark, PIN code, delivery time, price and order status.
  • Supplier details: business name, area and PIN code, materials, number of tractors, ready status, and for each load the rate, vehicle type and number, and mining e-challan number.
  • Precise vehicle location of suppliers who post a load and turn on live sharing, so buyers can see where the gadi is. A notification shows while sharing is on.
  • Buyer location is used on the phone only, to show nearby loads and distances. It is not stored on our servers.
  • Payment information is handled by our licensed payment partner. We receive the payment status and amount, not your card details or UPI PIN.

Who can see it

  • Phone numbers are not shown to other users. Buyers see a supplier code (for example "RN-482"), area, materials and a shortened vehicle number. Suppliers see the delivery address of orders given to them, not the buyer's number. Load Gadi staff can see numbers to arrange and support orders.
  • Data is stored with Google Firebase in Google's Mumbai, India region, and payments are processed by our payment partner. These companies process data on our behalf.
  • We may share information if required by law or by a government authority.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules limit each user to their own information. Loads not updated for 6 hours are hidden. Order and payment records are kept as long as required by tax and accounting law; other data is deleted when you ask us to delete your account.

Your choices and account deletion

  • You can turn off live location sharing or deny location permission at any time.
  • To delete your account, see Delete account or contact us with your registered mobile number. We delete the data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

Children

Load Gadi is a business service for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18.
