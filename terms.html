1. What Load Gadi does

Load Gadi is an online platform that connects buyers of balu (sand), gitti (stone chips) and bricks with local suppliers and vehicle owners in Bihar. Buyers place orders through the app or the Load Gadi helpline; Load Gadi confirms the order, passes it to a supplier, and helps until the material is delivered.

The material is sold and delivered by the supplier. Load Gadi is not the manufacturer, miner or transporter of the material. We act as the intermediary that arranges the order, collects payment on the supplier's behalf and pays the supplier after delivery.

  • Suppliers pay Load Gadi a liaison (service) fee on each order received through Load Gadi, as agreed with the supplier and shown in the app. It is deducted from the payout before the balance is paid to the supplier.
