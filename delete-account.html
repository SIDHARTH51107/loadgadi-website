Delete your Load Gadi account

App: Load Gadi – Balu Gitti Brick · Developer: Grihakriti Construction Pvt. Ltd., Patna, Bihar, India

Buyers do not have an account in Load Gadi, so there is nothing to delete for buyers. Sellers log in with their mobile number and can ask us to delete their account and all their data at any time.

How to request deletion

  • Step 1. Send an email to sidharthassociate@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Load Gadi account”.
  • Step 2. In the email, write the mobile number you use to log in to Load Gadi.
  • Step 3. We may call or message that number once to confirm the request is really from you.
  • Step 4. We delete your account and data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What we delete

  • Your seller login (mobile number and account ID) from Firebase Authentication.
  • Your seller profile, including your name and mobile number.
  • All loads you posted: material, quantity, rate, zone, vehicle number, e-challan number, and the vehicle locations saved with them.

What we keep

We do not keep any of your Load Gadi data after deletion, except where the law or a government authority requires us to keep it. In that case we keep only what is required, for only as long as required.

If you only want to remove a load, you can do it yourself in the app by marking it Sold. You can also turn off live location sharing at any time.
