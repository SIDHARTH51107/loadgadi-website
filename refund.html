Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Homelayer Infra Pvt. Ltd., Patna, Bihar, India

संक्षेप में: सप्लायर के निकलने से पहले ऑर्डर रद्द करें तो पूरा पैसा वापस। माल कम या ख़राब आए तो 24 घंटे के अंदर फ़ोटो के साथ बताएँ। रिफ़ंड उसी तरीक़े से (UPI/कार्ड/बैंक) 5–7 कार्य-दिवस में।

In short: cancel before the supplier is dispatched and you get a full refund. Report short or poor material within 24 hours with photos. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Cancelling an order

When you cancelWhat you get back
Before a supplier accepts the orderFull refund. You can cancel in the app ("Cancel order").
After the supplier accepts, before the vehicle leavesFull refund. Call or WhatsApp the Load Gadi helpline.
After the vehicle has left for your siteRefund minus the actual transport cost already incurred, which we will tell you before deducting.
After deliveryAn order cannot be cancelled after delivery; see "Problems with a delivery" below.

If the supplier cannot deliver, or Load Gadi cancels the order, you get a full refund, or we arrange another supplier if you prefer.

Problems with a delivery

  • Short quantity, wrong material or poor quality: tell us within 24 hours of delivery, with photos or a video of the load and, where possible, the measurement.
  • We check with the supplier and decide within 3 working days. Depending on what we find, we arrange the balance quantity, a replacement, or a partial or full refund of the affected part.
  • Online payments are held until delivery is confirmed, so reporting a problem before you give the delivery code to the driver is the fastest way to get it fixed.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds of online payments go back to the same payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet) you paid with.
  • Once approved, refunds are started within 2 working days and usually reach you within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • For cash orders, refunds are paid by UPI or bank transfer to the buyer's own account.

Suppliers

If a buyer receives a refund because of short quantity, wrong material or a failed delivery by the supplier, that amount is adjusted against the supplier's payout. Suppliers are paid after delivery is confirmed, minus Load Gadi's liaison fee.
